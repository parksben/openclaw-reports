## 计算方式

- 1 EUR = 6.78 ÷ 0.872 = 7.78 CNY
- 1 GBP = 6.78 ÷ 0.756 = 8.97 CNY
- 100 JPY = 6.78 ÷ 161.26 × 100 = 4.20 CNY
- 1 HKD = 6.78 ÷ 7.84 = 0.8648 CNY
